MINDSHIFT – Privacy Policy

App: MINDSHIFT (package com.mindshift.game) · Developer: G-FORCE 9.8 · Last updated: 7 October 2026

MINDSHIFT is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with information. The short version: MINDSHIFT does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not collect your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or device identifiers.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your own device:

  • your game progress (completed levels, stars, best times, Mind Coins, hints used);
  • your achievements and Daily Challenge status (streak, completion);
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, dark mode, daily reminder on/off, chosen language).

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to any server and is not accessible to the developer. You can erase the game progress at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app in Android settings.

3. Internet access

The app does not use the internet. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration – used for haptic feedback when you answer. It can be turned off in Settings.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later) – used only if you turn on the optional daily reminder in Settings. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device at about 6 PM when the Daily Challenge is waiting. You can switch it off at any time.
  • Receive boot completed – used only so that the optional daily reminder can be scheduled again after you restart your phone.

5. Third parties, advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, no social-media SDKs and no other third-party services that receive data. We do not share data with anyone because we do not collect any.

6. Children

The game is a general puzzle game and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data and has no accounts, advertising or online features, there is no information to delete or disclose.

7. Languages

The app can be used in English, Georgian, Russian, French, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Italian. The language you choose is stored only on your device. This policy applies to all language versions.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, by adding online features), this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date above will change.
